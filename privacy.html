Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how ASR (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Bloom app and this website. We collect only what we need to run Bloom, we never sell your personal information, and you can delete your account at any time.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, name and username. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the details you choose to share with us. You can also use Bloom as a guest without an account.
  • Profile details (optional): profile photo, short bio, birthday and gender.
  • Your goals and progress: goal titles, your answers to the coach's questions (for example an income range for a savings goal), daily steps (“petals”), completions, reflections and journal entries.
  • Circles: the Circles you start or join, Circle names, invite codes, and your progress on shared challenges. Members of a Circle can see each other's names and Circle progress.
  • Community posts: anything you choose to share publicly, which other Bloom users can see.
  • Messages to us: what you send through our contact form or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics: which screens and features are used (for example “goal created” or “paywall shown”), device type and app version. We use this to improve Bloom.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data so we can fix problems.
  • Reminders: notification reminders are scheduled on your device. We do not receive your notification content.

Purchases

Bloom+ is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your card details. We receive confirmation of your subscription status so we can unlock Bloom+ features.

AI-generated plans

To build your plan, Bloom sends your goal title, category and coach answers to our server, which uses OpenAI to generate suggested steps. We do not send your name or email for this. OpenAI processes this data on our behalf under its API terms.

How we use information

  • To run Bloom: save your goals, generate plans, sync across your devices, and power Circles and the journal.
  • To provide Bloom+ and handle your subscription status.
  • To keep Bloom safe, including moderating reported community content.
  • To understand and improve the app.
  • To reply to you when you contact us.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, analytics, diagnostics and cloud functions), Apple (sign-in and payments), Google Sign-In, and OpenAI (plan generation). They process data only to provide their services to us. Your Bloom data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the Middle East (Dammam, Saudi Arabia) and may be processed in other regions, including the United States, by these providers.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, with other users when you choose to (Circles and community posts), or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and its data in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Guest data stays on our servers only until you delete it or remove the app. Messages sent to us are kept as long as needed to respond.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and goals in the app at any time.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing us.

Children

Bloom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and access rules that restrict your data to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact
