Revenue engine 01 · Software

Bloom — subscription SaaS

Recurring revenue through the Apple App Store.

  • A free tier lets users feel real value before reaching a limit.
  • Bloom+ unlocks unlimited goals and AI plans — monthly, yearly, and lifetime.
  • Billing handled by StoreKit on the App Store.
  • Growth levers: onboarding conversion, referrals, retention.
Revenue engine 02 · Consumer brand

ASR Coffee — direct-to-consumer

Private-label product sold online, no middlemen.

  • Single-origin Ethiopia Yirgacheffe.
  • Range: capsules, whole beans, and a signature matte-black tumbler.
  • Direct-to-consumer — ordered online, shipped to the door.
  • Planned launch October 2026, starting in the GCC.
