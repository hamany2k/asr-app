Bloom

An AI-powered goal and habit tracker. Bloom’s ClarityCoach turns any goal into a clear, achievable plan through a focused 4-question flow — then grows it as a garden, where every daily action is a petal and every goal a chapter.

  • ClarityCoach — a 4-question engine that builds a SMART plan in under a minute.
  • Garden progress metaphor — watch goals bloom petal by petal.
  • Context-aware daily actions — short, specific, never vague.
  • Subscription model — free to start, Bloom+ to go unlimited.
